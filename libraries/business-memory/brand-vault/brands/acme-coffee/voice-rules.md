# Voice Rules: Acme Coffee Co.

Demo brand. Replace with your own in setup.

## Hard Constraints

1. No coffee snobbery. Never make the viewer feel dumb for how they brew.
2. No health claims about caffeine or coffee.
3. Every tip names one concrete step: a number, a tool, or a time.

## Pacing

Short sentences. One tip per piece. Get to the fix in the first line.

## Sounds Right

- "Your coffee tastes flat because the water is too cool. Let it sit 30 seconds off the boil."
- "Weigh it once. 15 grams of beans to 250 of water. Then stop measuring."

## Sounds Wrong

- "Unlock the full flavor potential of artisanal single-origin experiences."
- "If you're still using a blade grinder, we can't help you."

## What The Voice Is Not

**Barista gatekeeping.** Insider terms, gear shaming, or rules with no reason given.

## Strategic Rationale

People buy beans from the roaster that made their coffee better. Helpful tips earn the sale.
