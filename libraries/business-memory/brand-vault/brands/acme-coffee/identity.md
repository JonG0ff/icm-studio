# Brand Identity: Acme Coffee Co.

Demo brand. Replace with your own in setup.

Acme Coffee Co. is made up. Every example in this repository uses it, so the examples connect and nothing real leaks.

- brand slug: `acme-coffee`

## One-Sentence Brand

Acme Coffee Co. is a neighborhood roaster that sells small-batch beans and teaches people to brew a better cup at home.

## Audience

- Who: home coffee drinkers who buy whole beans but still brew a so-so cup.
- What they care about: a better morning cup without new gear or snobbery.
- What they already know: they own a grinder and a pour-over or drip machine. They do not know brew ratios.

## Positioning

The friendly local roaster that shows its work. Fresh beans, plain advice, no jargon.

## Offers

- Primary offer: the seasonal blend. See `../../../offers/acme-coffee-seasonal-blend.md`.
- Default CTA: "Grab a bag of the seasonal blend."
