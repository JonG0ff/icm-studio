# Offers

Approved offer records for your brands.

An offer record stores the link, the program terms, the compliance rules, the partner brand assets, and the placement list. One file per offer.

## Offer Routing

| Offer | Brand | Load | Status |
|-------|-------|------|--------|
| Seasonal blend | Acme Coffee Co. (demo) | `acme-coffee-seasonal-blend.md` | Demo offer. Replace in setup. |

## Rules

- An offer record stores facts and constraints. It does not store content direction.
- Check the compliance section before publishing anything that carries the link.
- Partner compliance rules outrank our own format preferences.
- Do not copy partner terms into brand or project files. Point here.
- Content direction lives in `../project-memory/projects/`.
- Format templates live in `../content-types/`.
