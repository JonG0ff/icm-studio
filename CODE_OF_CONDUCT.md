# Code Of Conduct

We want icm-studio to be a friendly place to learn and build.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to agents and Git.
- Give credit. Name the people and sources whose work you build on.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you assume.

## Not Accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or discrimination of any kind.
- Posting content you do not have the right to share.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through the repository's **Security** tab ("Report a vulnerability"). Say it is a conduct report. Reports stay private.

Maintainers may remove content, lock threads, or block people who break these rules.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
