# Script Lock Checklist

- The script follows the accepted objective, angle, messages, CTA, and constraints.
- The opening promise and its payoff are explicit.
- Short-form hooks follow the selected recipe; long-form chapters have a purpose, takeaway, and transition.
- Lines speak naturally for the intended delivery.
- Runtime and chapter totals fit the brief.
- On-screen emphasis and caption requirements are clear.
- Uncertain claims, quotations, dates, and source needs are flagged.
- Brand voice and approved business claims are respected.
- Existing locks and pending approvals remain intact.
