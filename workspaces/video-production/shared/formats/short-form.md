# Short-Form Video Recipe

Use the shared stages from brief through delivery. Load approved brand-specific reel SOPs only when they fit the request.

For footage-led edits, load the approved [footage-short quality SOP](../../../../libraries/business-memory/sops/footage-short-quality.md). It governs the hook/payoff, relevant interaction goal, purposeful graphics, caption accuracy, and full playback QA. Similar social edits primarily target Instagram/Reels unless the brief says otherwise.

- Brief: record placement, runtime, aspect ratio, safe zones, hook, payoff, relevant interaction goal, any justified CTA, and caption/export requirements. Default similar footage-led social edits to 9:16; the brief decides.
- Script: pair a visual and text hook for social reels. Make their promise clear in the first three seconds and identify the payoff beat.
- Scenes: use the selected retention guidance. Make visual changes carry the idea forward; do not add decorative resets to fill a cadence. Keep deliberate holds when they serve the story.
- Assets: use supplied footage where it carries the point. Source supporting candidates only for a named need; extra media is not a quality goal. A still-first shot brief uses the still-bank skill instead.
- Edit: check mobile text size, UI safe zones, sparse overlays, caption legibility, voice clarity, and platform export requirements.
- QA: complete full audiovisual playback of the final export before presenting it; inspect at mobile size and review muted comprehension where relevant. If playback cannot be performed, final-video QA is blocked, not ready.

For reel split tests, load `../../../../libraries/business-memory/sops/reel-split-test.md` together with `../../../../libraries/business-memory/sops/instagram-publishing-safety.md`. Keep the body and CTA fixed, rotate differentiation, and ship the whole cell. Results and queue operations remain under `../../publishing/`.
