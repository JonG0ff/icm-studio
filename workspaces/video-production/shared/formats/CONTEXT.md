# Video Formats

Load one recipe selected by the brief. The shared production stages remain the same.

| Format | Recipe | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Short form | `short-form.md` | Reels, shorts, concise social video, and reel split-test cells |
| Motion graphics, trial | `motion-graphics.md` | Graphic explainers, kinetic type, diagrams, and product presentations |
| Hybrid short, experimental | `hybrid-short.md` | One selected talking clip with captions, emphasis, supporting media, and source-to-output traceability |
| Long form | `long-form.md` | Tutorials, interviews, demonstrations, explainers, and chaptered video |

Duration alone does not set aspect ratio or platform. Record both in the brief. If the requested format needs a production method these recipes do not cover, name the gap before promising delivery.
