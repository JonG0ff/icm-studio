# Motion Graphics, Trial

Use for graphic explainers, kinetic-type videos, animated diagrams, and product presentations. The existing Video Production stages own the job. The shared `remotion/` runtime hosts implementation.

Load `../../../../skills/ai-capabilities/design/motion-graphic-design/SKILL.md` for direction and the canonical Remotion skill for APIs. This recipe is trial. Creative acceptance and full playback QA of a real job are required before calling it live.

- Brief: capture one message, audience, delivery, brand, references, audio, and review mode.
- Script: state exact screen copy and distinguish it from spoken words and captions.
- Scene plan: define the focal point and the graphic relationship each beat explains. Record timing and reading holds before assembly.
- Assets: prefer original geometry, approved brand assets, and selected licensed modules. Record font, code, music, and media sources before edit lock.
- Build: reuse small typed modules. Keep job content out of shared code. Load fonts and drive motion from the frame timeline.
- Preview: inspect real composition frames, including transitions and final holds, at delivery and phone sizes before a full render.
- QA: complete full audiovisual playback. Record unavailable playback as blocked. Keep acceptance, library promotion, and publication decisions explicit.

The experimental shared kit is documented in `remotion/src/components/motion-graphics/README.md`. It does not require character, persona, rig, or voice stages for a graphic-only job.
