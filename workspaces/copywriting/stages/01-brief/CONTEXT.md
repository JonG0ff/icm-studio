# Stage 01: Brief

Capture the copy request and writing plan inside Copywriting.

## Inputs

| Source | Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| User | Request or brought copy/spec | Full | Goal and constraints |
| Template | `../../../../_core/templates/content-brief/brief-format.md` | Relevant fields | Shared planning method |
| Libraries | `../../../../libraries/business-memory/CONTEXT.md` and `../../../../libraries/swipes/CONTEXT.md` | Selected entries only | Approved facts, voice, references |

## Process

1. Capture slug, owner, platform/placement, audience, objective, angle, length, offer, CTA, deadline, and must-include/avoid constraints.
2. Load the selected brand/project context and reference sections. Record claim sources and rights limits.
3. Use the copywriting skill listed in `../../../../skills/README.md`. Its upstream helper names are not shipped here: use Business Memory for marketing context and `../03-review/CONTEXT.md` for copy editing. For email and social drafts, capture subject lines, preview text, sequence position, platform, and length as applicable. Use this workspace's stages through delivery; no separate helper skill is required.
4. Record review mode, writing plan, missing inputs, and pending decisions. Existing accepted inputs keep their approvals; do not infer new acceptance.
5. Save the brief and present direction under the job's review mode, then proceed to writing.

## Checkpoints

| After Step | Agent Presents | Human Decides | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Brief, angle, context gaps, and writing plan | Accept direction or revise | Taste, plan |
