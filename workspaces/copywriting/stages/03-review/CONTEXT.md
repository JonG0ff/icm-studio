# Stage 03: Review And Deliver

Review the complete copy against its brief and provide a clear delivery state.

## Inputs

| Source | Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brief | `../01-brief/output/[slug]/brief.md` | Full | Approved direction and constraints |
| Draft | `../02-write/output/[slug]/copy.md` | Full | Text to review |
| Sources | Brief pointers | Claims and voice constraints | Verification |

## Process

1. Check objective, angle, audience, voice, claims, length, links, offer, and CTA.
2. Resolve editorial issues within the accepted brief. Keep material claim/approval gaps explicit.
3. Update the copy file and save review notes with findings, source checks, `ready`, `ready-after-fixes`, or `blocked`, and pending decisions.
4. Present the copy and review notes under the job's review mode. Record human acceptance only when actually given. Sending or publishing is a separate action.

## Checkpoints

| After Step | Agent Presents | Human Decides | Kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Reviewed copy, remaining issues, delivery state, and exact next action | Accept or request revisions | Ship |

## Audit

| Check | Pass Condition |
|---|---|
| Complete | Requested copy and review notes exist |
| Verified | Claims, voice, constraints, and source limits checked |
| Honest state | Blockers and pending acceptance explicit |

## Outputs

| Artifact | Location | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Review notes | `output/[slug]/review.md` | Findings, state, pending decisions, next action |
| Reviewed copy | `../02-write/output/[slug]/copy.md` | Complete text and separate source notes |
