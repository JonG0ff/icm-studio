Make and review a text deliverable directly in its owning workspace.

## Rules

- Use the shipped copywriting skill for pages, posts, captions, and email drafts. Its upstream helper-skill names are not installed dependencies here.
- Business Memory supplies marketing context. Stage 03 supplies copy editing. Capture channel-specific needs in the brief instead of searching for absent email or social skills.
- The request enters here; no separate intake/planning workspace or tracking card is required.
- Use one slug, selected context, and the review mode recorded in the brief.
- Existing accepted draft/spec decisions retain their approval status.
- Keep facts and claims traceable. Flag uncertainty and missing approvals.
- Delivery is copy-only. Publishing, email sending, and visual production require their own authorized task.
