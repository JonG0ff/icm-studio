# Platform Specs

Size, frame rate and length per platform. Format recipes pick a row.

## Targets

| Platform | Size | FPS | Length | Aspect |
|----------|------|-----|--------|--------|
| Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | 1080x1920 | 30 | 15-90 s | 9:16 |
| YouTube long-form | 1920x1080 | 30 | 6-12 min | 16:9 |
| X, LinkedIn feed | 1080x1350 | 30 | 15-90 s | 4:5 |

This workspace defaults to Instagram Reels at 30 fps.

## Safe Areas (9:16)

- Keep faces and text out of the top 250 px and bottom 400 px.
- Keep text out of the right 120 px. Platform buttons sit there.

## Pace

- ElevenLabs dialogue runs about 150-180 words per minute with pauses.
- Comedy holds add time. Budget 10-15% for holds and buttons.

## Render Settings

- Codec: H.264 mp4.
- Phone copy: same size, lower bitrate, for review on a phone.
