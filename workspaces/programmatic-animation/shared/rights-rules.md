# Rights Rules

Hard lines. Every stage that makes or brings content checks these.

## Art

- Original characters, sets and props only.
- References are for studying a style. Never trace or copy a frame.
- AI image generation is for roughs. Clean art is redrawn or rebuilt in code.
- Paper-collage photos and textures: our own photos, generated textures, or properly licensed images only.

## Voices

- Never clone a real person's voice. ElevenLabs prohibits it.
- Parody a type, not a person. Example: "generic anchor with too much hair gel".
- No real names of private people in scripts.

## Clips

- Never repost someone else's viral clip or sports footage. It gets claimed or removed.
- Re-enact it in our own animation instead. We own every frame.
- A re-enactment can be deliberately bad. That's often the joke.

## Music and SFX

- Every file gets a rights note: source, license, link, date.
- Notes live in the run's `sfx-map.md`.
- Use the `media-sourcing` and `sound-effects-mix` skills for sourcing and license rows.

## Brought Files

- Files you bring pass the same rules.
- If the source is unknown, the stage flags it and waits.
