# Specification Fields

Use these questions inside the destination workspace's brief when a marketing deliverable needs more direction. The completed brief stays with its job. No separate spec or routing workspace is required.

- Objective: What should this accomplish for the brand/project?
- Audience: Who is it for, and what do they know?
- Angle: What single idea should they remember?
- Key messages: Which three to five points matter most?
- Content direction: What opening, body structure, offer, CTA, and payoff serve the objective?
- Visual/audio direction: What approved mood, colors, type, voice, and format guidance apply?
- References: Which library entries and pattern sections informed the choices?
- Constraints: What must be included, avoided, sourced, or approved?
- Success criteria: How will the result be judged?
- Experiment, if requested: What hypothesis, test stage, primary metric, and read plan apply?

For reel split tests, the brief carries the hypothesis and fixed body/CTA; production creates hook packages and the variant matrix. For copy-only work, the Copywriting workspace owns the complete draft.

Existing accepted specs remain valid job inputs at their migrated locations. Preserve their wording and approvals; do not force them into a new template.
